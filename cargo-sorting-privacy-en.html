Cargo Sorting 3D — Privacy Policy

Operator: LumoNest Games. Contact: lumonestgames@gmail.com. Effective date: 3 November 2026. Package: com.independentharbor.cargosorting3d.

About the game

Cargo Sorting 3D is a cargo-sorting puzzle game intended for teenagers aged 13 and over and adults. There are no player accounts, chat, uploads, real-money purchases or developer-operated analytics/cloud-save servers.

Information on your device

Progress, puzzle sessions, star ratings, coins, boosters, port upgrades, daily rewards, language, sound, graphics and notification settings are saved locally. These game files are not sent to a developer server. Recovery copies of the local save may be kept to recover damaged saves. Clearing app storage or uninstalling normally removes local game data. Android or device backup services may retain their own copies under their settings; we cannot remotely access or delete them.

Age groups

Before advertising is enabled, a neutral one-time screen asks for a group: under 13, 13–15, 16–17, or 18 and over. We do not request a birth date or name. The selected group stays in the local save and determines advertising treatment. Advertising and consent requests are disabled for the under-13 group. Ages 13–15 receive restricted under-age-of-consent treatment; ages 16–17 receive teen treatment. Adult ad requests follow the applicable consent choices. The advertising SDK receives the corresponding treatment signals, rather than your locally saved game progress.

Advertising

The Android app integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads Unity Plugin 11.6.0, Android SDK 25.5.0) and Google User Messaging Platform 4.0.0. A banner may appear in menus after the tutorial in space reserved below controls. It is hidden during puzzles and pause/outcome dialogs. An interstitial may appear when proceeding after four completed levels, subject to availability and a cooldown, never during a move or at cold launch. Optional rewarded ads grant boosters or coins only after the SDK confirms the reward. Playing and saving do not require watching ads. Gameplay works offline; ads need a connection.

Data handled by Google

Depending on age treatment, consent and SDK operation, Google may receive network information including the IP address and approximate location inferred from it, advertising or other device identifiers, ad/app interactions and technical diagnostics. These support ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. The app does not request precise GPS location, camera, microphone, contacts or access to your photo library. We do not send Google player names, email addresses, puzzle saves or port layouts. Advertising communication uses encryption in transit. Local information depends on device security.

Consent and privacy options

UMP checks applicable consent requirements before advertising can initialize. Where required, it presents the publisher’s privacy message and provides a way to revisit privacy choices in Settings → Privacy. Ages 13–15 are marked under the age of consent in both UMP and ad treatment; the app does not ask them to consent as adults. Personalization for eligible adult users depends on their choices and the applicable Google settings. A consent choice does not prevent offline play.

Notifications and support

Optional local daily reminders are disabled by default; Android permission is requested only after you turn them on. The developer does not operate push-notification servers. If you contact lumonestgames@gmail.com, the email and information you voluntarily provide are used to answer the request and may be retained as needed to resolve it or meet applicable obligations. Please avoid unnecessary personal information.

Retention, deletion and rights

LumoNest Games does not retain your game saves on a server and cannot remotely restore or erase them. You can clear local app storage through Android settings. Google applies its own retention rules to information it receives. Contact us about applicable access, deletion or privacy rights; requests about Google advertising data may also require contacting Google. There is no in-game account to delete.

Third parties and changes
