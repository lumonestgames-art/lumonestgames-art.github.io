Privacy Policy

Idle Planet Reshaper · LumoNest Games
Last updated: November 12, 2026

Game progress is stored locally. There are no accounts or cloud saves. Optional advertising and privacy choices are handled through Google AdMob and UMP.

1. Who operates the game

This policy explains how information is handled in Idle Planet Reshaper, an Android game by LumoNest Games. For support and privacy questions, contact lumonestgames@gmail.com. Developer website: lumonestgames-art.github.io. This policy applies to this game; other LumoNest Games products may have separate policies.

2. Local progress and settings

The game stores your terraforming progress, energy, buildings, research, expedition data, offline-income timestamps, reward records and settings, including language, on your device. These files let you continue playing and help prevent duplicate rewards. We do not upload them to our own server. There is no registration or cloud save, and no separate gameplay analytics service is enabled. Advertising services perform their own measurement as described below.

The atlas is also stored only on your device. Older saves may retain inactive appearance fields for compatibility; the current version has no appearance menu. This version has no real-money purchases, subscriptions, paid ad bypass or purchase-verification server. Rewarded ads require an explicit player choice; base offline income is credited automatically without an ad.

3. Google AdMob and UMP

The game uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage privacy choices. The advertising SDK may process your IP address and approximate location, device/advertising identifiers, interactions with the game and ads, and diagnostic information. Purposes include ad delivery, measurement, operation and fraud prevention. Processing depends on SDK version, configuration and privacy choices. Details: Google Mobile Ads data disclosure, Google Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising choices and consent

You can play the main game without being required to watch ads. Loading ads requires internet access. Where required, a privacy form appears before the relevant advertising processing. Where UMP provides a privacy-options entry point for your region, use “Settings → Privacy Options” to change your choices. Withdrawing consent affects future processing without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. Declining personalization does not necessarily stop all technical processing: non-personalized or limited ads may be available in accordance with your choices and the service rules. Android also provides controls for your advertising identifier.

5. Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and handle support or privacy requests. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Our mailbox uses Gmail, which is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Purposes and grounds for processing

Local game data enables the game’s features. Support information is used to provide the help you request, handle disputes and protect the service. Where GDPR or similar rules apply, relevant grounds include providing a requested service, legitimate interests in support and security subject to your rights, and applicable legal obligations. Advertising processing that requires consent relies on the choices expressed through the privacy form. Playing the game does not by itself constitute consent to personalized advertising.

7. Recipients and international processing

Advertising information may be received by Google and advertising participants identified in the privacy message and service documentation. We do not sell your local progress file. Advertising disclosures may qualify as “sharing” or other regulated transfers under some regional laws; available advertising controls depend on those rules. Google, email and hosting services may process information outside your country using safeguards described in their policies. Details: Google Privacy Policy, GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Retention and deletion

Local files remain while needed for the game or until you clear the app’s data through Android or uninstall the app. Doing so may permanently erase your progress; we do not hold a server copy to restore it. SDK or system settings may have separate lifecycles. We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request, reasonable follow-up issues and applicable legal obligations. Google’s data is subject to its own retention and deletion practices; clearing the game does not automatically erase information already received by Google.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location and the processing involved, you may have rights to information, access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent and a complaint to a relevant supervisory authority. Contact lumonestgames@gmail.com; we may request only information necessary to verify your request. For information separately controlled by Google, also use Google’s tools and contacts. We cannot remotely read or erase a local save on your device. More about EU rights: European Commission.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures and limit processing to its stated purposes. Local-file protection also depends on your device’s security. Google reports encryption in transit for advertising SDK data; no storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security.

11. Children

The game is intended for teenagers aged 13 and older and adults, and is not directed at children under 13. The age required for consent may differ by country; a 13+ label does not replace those requirements. If you believe a child’s information has been handled improperly, contact us so that we can review the issue and take appropriate action.

12. Website, external links and changes

These policy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add our own analytics trackers to them; the host may process technical request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. External websites and advertising destinations have their own policies. We may update this policy when features or requirements change; the last-updated date appears above. Where a change requires a new notice or consent, we will provide it through an appropriate mechanism. For questions about this policy, contact lumonestgames@gmail.com.
