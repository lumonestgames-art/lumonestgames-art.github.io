Privacy policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026
lumonestgames@gmail.com

About this policy

Gravity Shift is an Android arcade game operated by LumoNest Games. Privacy and support contact: lumonestgames@gmail.com. This policy applies to this game, including its local progress, age-group choice and optional advertising. It does not describe LumiCubes or other games.

Information stored on your device

The game stores records, coin balance, upgrades, skins, challenge and tutorial progress, language, sound and vibration preferences, and your selected age group in the app’s private storage. It stores an age band, not a name or date of birth. Save files can include a previous-save backup and local recovery copies. Our game code does not upload these files to a developer-operated game server. There are no player accounts, chat, user uploads, real-money purchases or developer-operated analytics servers in this game.

Age groups and children

The game is intended for a mixed audience. Before the advertising flow, users select an age group on a neutral screen: under 13, 13–17, 18 or older, or prefer not to say. The selection is saved locally and can be changed in Settings. The game does not request a precise date of birth. For users below 18, users who prefer not to say, and users whose age is unknown, the game’s advertising code does not request ads or start the Google consent flow. Core gameplay, tutorial, coin progression and challenges remain available without ads. Advertising is eligible only for users who select 18 or older, and is additionally subject to Google’s privacy checks and ad availability.

Advertising for eligible adult users

The game integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). A banner may appear in the main menu, results or shop, away from controls. An interstitial may appear at a natural break between runs, with run-count and time limits. Ads are not shown during active gameplay or the tutorial; interstitials and banners are also suppressed in the first session. Optional rewarded ads can provide one continue, double run coins or grant a shop coin bonus. Rewards require the SDK’s earned-reward callback. Watching ads is not required for core progression. The app requests a maximum ad content rating of G; this request does not replace the age screen or privacy controls.

Data handled by Google

When the advertising flow is eligible and permitted, ad and consent requests communicate with Google. Depending on SDK operation and privacy settings, Google may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), ad and app interactions, technical diagnostics, and device or other identifiers for advertising delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Limited or non-personalised advertising can still involve processing data. Our game does not provide Google with your save contents, name or date of birth. It does not request GPS location, camera, microphone, contacts or access to your photos. Google’s processing and retention are described in Google’s Privacy Policy, Google advertising information and the Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Consent and your choices

For eligible adult users, the app checks UMP privacy status at launch and presents a consent form when required. It requests ads only when UMP indicates requests are permitted. Where required, Settings → Privacy settings reopens Google’s privacy options so you can review or withdraw choices. A network or consent error does not block core gameplay. You can select “Prefer not to say” in the age-group settings to disable the game’s advertising flow. Changing a local choice does not automatically delete information already processed by Google; Google’s controls and request procedures also apply.

Retention, deletion and recovery

Local game data and recovery copies remain on the device until replaced, cleared through Android app settings, or removed when the app is uninstalled. These actions may permanently remove progress. Device or operating-system backup services may retain separate backups under their own settings; we cannot remotely retrieve or erase those backups. We do not retain game saves on a developer server. Google controls retention of information it receives under its own policies. Support correspondence may be retained as necessary to answer a request, resolve an issue or meet applicable legal obligations; it is not used by us for advertising.

Parents, requests and rights

Parents, guardians and other users can contact lumonestgames@gmail.com about privacy, accidental personal information, or applicable rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and withdrawal of consent. Rights depend on the applicable law and the data controller involved. Please send only the information necessary to handle the request, and avoid sending a child’s personal details unless needed. Requests concerning Google’s systems may also need to be directed to Google. Where applicable, you may contact your data-protection supervisory authority.

Security and external pages

Local files are subject to device security, and online services use their own security safeguards. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Opening this privacy page in a browser contacts GitHub Pages, our hosting provider, which may handle technical request information under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. This policy site does not include our own analytics scripts or advertising.

Policy updates

We will update this page when the game’s data practices change and revise the effective date. Contact LumoNest Games at lumonestgames@gmail.com with questions.
